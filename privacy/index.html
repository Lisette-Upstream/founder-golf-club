Privacy

Privacy policy

We keep this simple: we only use your details to run the club.

Who we are

Founder Golf Club is a community for tech founders in the Netherlands, organised by Mila Jovićević and Lisette Braakenburg van Backum. Questions about your data go to info@foundergolfclub.nl.

What we collect

What you fill in on the application form: your name, email address, phone number, company details (stage and industry) and golf level.

Why we use it

  • To review your application.
  • To invite you to events and share event details.
  • To add you to the members' WhatsApp group, if you agree to that on the form.

We use your details only with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

The application form runs on Notion, and the members' group runs on WhatsApp. They store data for us under their own privacy terms. We don't share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For as long as you're part of the community. If you leave, or your application isn't accepted, we delete your details.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to be removed from the community, at any time. Email info@foundergolfclub.nl. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

This website

This website doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics.
