How

  1. Step 1

    Apply

    Fill in the form with your golf level, company stage, industry and email. It also asks for your consent for email and the WhatsApp group.

  2. Step 2

    See what's coming up

    Events are announced on Luma and in the WhatsApp group.

  3. Step 3

    Join an event

    Once you're in, events and ways to sign up for them will be shared with you as they happen, including details like dress code and timings.

House rules

  • Treat everyone with respect, whatever their stage, background or golf level.
  • What's said in the group stays in the group, unless the person says otherwise.
  • No hard selling or pitching. This is a community, not a sales event.
  • No harassment, discrimination or inappropriate behaviour.
  • Respect the club's rules, staff and facilities.
  • WhatsApp is for event logistics, intros and community updates. No spam or unsolicited promotion.

The organisers can remove a member for breaking these rules.

Privacy

We use the details in your application only to run the community, based on the consent you give in the form and in line with the GDPR. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or be removed from the community, at any time. Email us at lisette@unfounded.nl.
