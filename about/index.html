About

The founders behind the club.

Founder Golf Club is organised by two founders who wanted a more relaxed way to meet peers than another panel or round of drinks.

Mila Jovićević-Milica

Head of AI House at Prosus

Mila has spent nearly a decade building networks and scaling tech ventures. She has supported more than 100 founders and 80 Series A+ companies across talent, capital and operations. With an award-winning background in the arts, four languages and homes in London, New York, Amsterdam, Vienna and Belgrade behind her, what she enjoys most is bringing people together.

Mila on LinkedIn

Lisette Braakenburg van Backum

Co-founder & CEO, Upstream Festival

Lisette has been part of the Dutch startup and scale-up ecosystem for more than a decade, through initiatives such as Up!Rotterdam, House of Founders, and for the past seven years as co-founder and CEO of Upstream Festival. What gives her the most energy is bringing founders together, whether over dinner, on a trip or on the golf course.
