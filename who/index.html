Founder Golf Club
What Who How

Who

Core members are tech founders at seed stage and beyond. You qualify with any one of these.

You've raised a seed round
You're generating revenue
You have market share equivalent to a seed-stage company

Why seed and beyond?

We curate the group so members are at a similar stage and face similar challenges. That makes conversations more relevant and valuable.

Sector and location

All sectors are welcome, and founders can be based anywhere in the Netherlands. Events rotate across clubs and regions. If the group becomes big enough, we can consider industry groups too.

Golf level

All levels are welcome. Beginners are expected to take lessons in their own time, with the intention to join on the course within a reasonable time.

Guests

We start as a founder-first community. Investors and C-suite leaders with equity or ownership and a major role in the leadership team are invited on occasion, per event.

Plus-ones

There are no general plus-ones. Members can always recommend other founders, who then apply via the form.
