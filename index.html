Etiquette

House rules.

  • Everyone plays the same course.

    Respect for every stage, background and golf level.

  • What's said in the group stays in the group.

    Unless the person who shared it says otherwise.

  • The only pitch here is with a wedge.

  • Harassment is out of bounds.

  • We're guests on their course.

    Respect the club's rules, staff and facilities.

  • Keep the group chat on the fairway.

    WhatsApp is for logistics, intros and updates. No spam.

The organisers can remove a member for breaking these rules.

Q&A

Does everyone who applies get in?

No. We review every application and keep the group to founders at a similar stage. We'll let you know by email either way.

How often do you meet?

Four times a year, at a different golf club each time, somewhere in the Netherlands.

Do I need a handicap?

No, all levels are welcome. Some clubs ask for a golf proficiency certificate (GVB) or a handicap to play the course. We share the requirements with each event.

I'm a beginner. Can I still join?

Yes. We arrange driving range sessions where beginners can practise together. We don't provide lessons, so we ask beginners to take them in their own time. That way you can eventually join us on the course.

What does it cost?

The price depends on the club and is shared with each event. You pay when you sign up, which confirms your spot.

Can my company pay?

Yes. We can invoice your company.

Can I bring a co-founder or a friend?

There are no general plus-ones. Co-founders and friends who meet the criteria can apply themselves, and members can always recommend other founders.

Do investors join?

We're founder-first. Now and then we invite investors, and C-suite leaders with equity or ownership and a major leadership role, to join a specific event. Invitation only.

Where do I hear about new events?

By email and in the members' WhatsApp group, together with details like timings, dress code and cancellation terms.
